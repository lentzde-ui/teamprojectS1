Planning a budget for Ireland depends on your travel style, accommodations, and itinerary choices. The currency used in Ireland (Republic of Ireland) is the Euro (€), while Northern Ireland uses the Pound Sterling (£). Understanding typical travel costs ahead of time ensures you can experience everything Ireland has to offer without unexpected financial surprises.

Estimated Daily Budgets

Budget Backpacker

€70 – €100 / day

Hostel dorm beds, public bus travel, self-catering or casual pub meals, and free outdoor attractions.

Mid-Range Traveler

€160 – €260 / day

3-star hotels or cozy B&Bs, rented car or train rides, sit-down lunches and dinners, and paid site entry tickets.

Luxury Travel

€380+ / day

4-star/5-star boutique hotels or historic castle stays, fine dining restaurants, private guided tours, and car rentals with full insurance.

Family Travel

€300 – €450 / day

Family suites or Airbnb rentals, self-driving van rentals, child discounts on attractions, and combination dining options.

Key Trip Expense Categories

Accommodation Costs

Accommodation is often the largest portion of a travel budget in Ireland:

  • Hostels: €25 – €50 per night for a dorm bed in Dublin; €20 – €35 in smaller towns.
  • Bed & Breakfasts (B&Bs): €80 – €140 per night for double rooms including traditional breakfast.
  • Mid-Range Hotels: €130 – €240 per night depending on location and season.

Food & Dining Costs

Meals range from quick deli sandwiches to traditional pub dinners:

  • Deli Counter Lunch: €6 – €10 for a fresh chicken roll, soup, or sandwich.
  • Pub Dinner: €16 – €25 for classic Irish stew, fish and chips, or a burger.
  • Pint of Guinness: €5.50 – €8.00 depending on city and pub location.

Transportation Costs

Navigating between cities and rural scenic routes:

  • Car Rental: €30 – €70 per day (gas/petrol & manual vs automatic transmission affects rates).
  • Intercity Trains (Irish Rail): €15 – €35 single fare when booked online in advance.
  • City Transport: €2 – €3 per bus or tram ride using a Leap Card discount pass.

Attractions & Sightseeing

Entry fees for top historical and natural landmarks:

  • Cliffs of Moher: €7 – €12 (cheaper when reserved online in advance).
  • Blarney Castle & Gardens: €18 – €22 adult entry.
  • OPW Heritage Pass: €40 adult pass giving access to over 40 national monuments.

Smart Budgeting Insights

Top Money-Saving Tips for Ireland

1. Travel Shoulder Season Visit in April–May or September–October for lower flight rates and cheaper lodging.
2. Get an OPW Heritage Card Offers unlimited access to state-managed castles, national parks, and historic sites.
3. Buy a Visitor Leap Card Saves up to 30% on Dublin Bus, Luas trams, and DART trains compared to cash fares.
4. Lunch Specials & Early Birds Many restaurants offer lower-priced lunch or "Early Bird" set menus before 6:30 PM.
